Junhui Lin

Hi! I'm a first-year M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park. Before joining UMD, I studied Artificial Intelligence at Beijing Normal University in Beijing, China, where I developed a broad interest in computer vision, multimodal learning, and generative models.

My current interests lie in vision/multimodal learning, image/video generation, and robot learning. In the long term, I hope to develop intelligent systems that can efficiently learn from large-scale visual data, reason across modalities, and interact with complex real-world environments.
